# Crizo changelog

Every published version of Crizo for Android, newest first. Download any of them from
[Releases](https://github.com/CRIZA-LABS/crizo-app/releases); the latest is always at
[crizo.criza.dev/download](https://crizo.criza.dev/download).

## 0.10.10 (27 November 2026)

- Archive and Undo work from every page: archive or delete a memory and Undo shows right away, wherever you are.
- Archived memories are kept in Brain, Library, Archive. Open one and tap Unarchive to bring it back.

## 0.10.9 (27 November 2026)

- New Bin: anything you delete in Brain or Money stays 30 days. Undo right away, or restore it from the Bin.
- Find the Bin in Settings, the ⋯ menu, Brain's Library and Money's Sections.

## 0.10.8 (26 November 2026)

- The Places map loads its map squares again.
- Memories save your exact place now. Turn it off in Settings, Privacy.
- Every new memory gets its place when location is on: typed, photo, voice, list or from chat.
- The memory map graph is drawn by Crizo himself, so it shows on every phone. Pinch to zoom, tap to open.
- Voice notes: record your own voice with pause and resume, play it back in the memory, and Crizo writes the words, a title and tags on your phone.
- Delete, Archive, Share, Pin and the other actions in a memory are clear buttons now, not tags.
- Chat shows Crizo thinking when you ask about a memory.
- Tap Crizo on his tab to dress him up. Hold him to hear hello.
- No ad services or advertising ID in the app.

## 0.10.7 (26 November 2026)

- Undo works everywhere in memories: Undo and Redo buttons in the editor, and Undo after swiping, deleting, archiving, moving or merging stays in view.
- The memory editor has a full toolbar: headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, lists, quote, link, checklist, voice, photo, camera and tag, with bigger buttons.
- Crizo suggests a reminder when a memory names a time, adds tags you already use, and sums up long memories in one line. All on your phone.
- Auto tags are accurate: no more stray #code, and only words the memory really talks about.
- The memory map graph draws again, and tapping a memory opens it.
- Swipe between tabs in the memory map and in chat history.
- Play and the bedtime story in Extras work for every character, and say why if they cannot play.
- Empty Trash in one tap, a clear button in search, and the Sticker book is gone.

## 0.10.6 (25 November 2026)

- Collections in Brain: your memories are grouped automatically by tag, place, photos, voice notes and lists. Tap one to see them together.
- Tidy up: Crizo spots memories that say the same thing and payments saved twice, and merges them when you say so. Merged memories go to Trash, payments can be undone.

## 0.10.5 (25 November 2026)

- Kiko, Momo, Bolt, Mochi and Wisp are redrawn: shaded and glossy, with arms, feet, tails and little details, and clearer faces for every mood.
- Ears and antennae now sway with the body instead of sliding with the feet.
- Colour skins match their names on every character: Sky is sky blue on Kiko, Momo and Bolt alike, and Wisp takes a soft tint.
- Hats, ears and antennae are no longer cut off at the top: every outfit now shows in full on every character.
- Switching characters no longer flashes the old Crizo for a moment.

## 0.10.4 (25 November 2026)

- Spin or shake your character and it gets properly dizzy: swirly eyes and stars circling its head.
- Characters look 3D now, with soft light and shade in their own colours, and are much sharper. The green outline on every character is gone.
- Wear several accessories at once: a hat, glasses, a scarf and a hair clip. Browse them by category, or pick a ready-made Combo.
- Snack time and pet time use your live character: it watches the snack, chomps with crumbs, and floats hearts where you stroke.
- Settings previews play their sound, and the preview Crizo stays in view while you scroll.
- Tabs land on the one you tapped, even when tapping quickly, and swiping is equally easy both ways.
- The + button shows only with the tab bar. The menu has Ask Crizo, Extras and Sticker book.
- The hold menu can add a voice note, a photo memory or a payment, or ask Crizo, from anywhere.
- Classic look applies to Crizo only.

## 0.10.3 (25 November 2026)

- The hold menu has a new Add memory action you can put on it, opening the app straight to a new memory. Feed and pet lines now fit each character.
- Daily care: feed your character a snack (each one has a favourite), stroke it until it is in love, and play a game. Doing all three grows your care streak.
- Finger play: tickle with small wiggles to make your character giggle, shake it for a dizzy reaction that grows if you keep going, or draw circles to spin it. Each character reacts in its own words and voice.
- Extras page refreshed, and the old duplicate daily gift removed.
- New Sticker maker: your character, in its outfit, with the face and words you choose. Send it in any chat or save it to Photos.
- The home screen widget, chat and mini-game icons now show the character you picked.
- Each character has its own cute voice: Kiko coos, Momo mews and purrs, Bolt beeps, Mochi bloops and Wisp hums.
- Sound previews work on the first tap, and Room echo now works with every voice.
- Silent, vibrate and Do Not Disturb always win: Crizo makes no sound then, and the Sounds page tells you why.
- The centre button is now Add: a memory, list, voice note, photo or payment in one tap. Wake and rest Crizo from the same sheet.
- Easier Settings: movement lives in Customize, and Quiet hours switches on right in the list. Demos play on Crizo where you can see him.
- Tap your character on the Crizo tab or in Customize to hear it say hello.
- Four new companions: Momo the sleepy cat, Bolt the busy robot, Mochi the bouncy jelly and Wisp the dreamy ghost. Each one looks and behaves in its own way.
- 16 new accessories, 27 in all, from halos and bunny ears to a Viking helmet. Every accessory now fits every character, and the headphones sit properly on the sides of the head.
- Shop items now show your character wearing each one, so you see exactly how it looks.
- Security: with the private lock on, Crizo blocks screenshots and hides its preview in recent apps. Modified copies of the app are detected.
- Customize is now a dressing room: Crizo stays big on stage while you change his character, look, outfits and moves in tabs below. Open it from Customize on Crizo's card.
- Every accessory shows its own icon, and early users can wear them all.
- A daily surprise from Crizo: a memory from this day, his adventure postcard, a gift sticker or a moment to write something down.
- Google Drive backup is paused for now. Save backup can still go to your Drive through the file picker.
- Meet Kiko: a new, sky-blue companion who is calm and curious. He floats, thinks and keeps you company. Pick him in Crizo Shop, Characters.

## 0.10.2 (25 November 2026)

- Places map: see your memories as pins on a real map (Brain, Places, Map). Only map squares load from the internet; your memories stay on the phone.
- Crizo has moods now: after you chat, or while charging, his on-phone brain picks a mood for the next few hours (playful, calm, caring, curious or sleepy), and he behaves that way.

## 0.10.1 (25 November 2026)

- Memories: headings, underline and strikethrough, Find in memory with next and previous, drag checklist items into any order, and Share, Copy text and created/edited info in the menu.
- Crizo Shop in Crizo's page: name your companion, and dress him in 8 new accessories (crown, beanie, cap, top hat, headphones, sunglasses, bow tie, flower). Earn some with your streak and stickers.
- A cleaner, more premium look: proper icons instead of emoji across Brain, Money and Chat.
- Setup now also helps with battery (so Crizo keeps floating and reminders arrive on time) and with in-app updates.
- Google Drive backup in Settings, Backup: a hidden, encrypted copy in your own Drive, daily while charging if you like. Crizo never sees your other Drive files.
- Smarter chat: Crizo finds the right memories by meaning as well as words, understands yesterday, last week or in October, and shows when each memory was saved.
- Answers come first and stay grounded in your notes and money. If something is not in your notes, Crizo says so instead of guessing.
- Plain-words actions understand amounts like 1.2k or ₹450, Hinglish like kal raat, and use the amount you typed.
- Payments sort into categories more accurately, with Indian shops and apps.
- Tapping the update notification starts the update straight away.
- Send feedback writes the email for you, with your Crizo and Android version and phone model, so problems get fixed faster. Never your memories or money.
- Share Crizo in About sends a friend the link.
- After five days, Crizo asks once how it is going. Love it, share it; anything else goes straight to me.

## 0.10.0 (24 November 2026)

- Smoother tabs: swiping no longer redraws the whole app halfway, and tapping a tab glides straight there.
- The Crizo button is your quick actions: tap for a new memory, list, voice, photo, payment or Ask Crizo. Hold it to wake or rest Crizo.
- Quick entry has its own Save button inside the box, and the keyboard's Done key saves too.
- The hold menu on New and Add closes by itself when you let go.
- A short first-run tour with Skip, shown once.
- Optional, anonymous usage statistics, off unless you turn them on in Settings, Privacy. Never your memories, money or messages.

## 0.9.9 (24 November 2026)

- Tabs: tapping Crizo, Brain or Money always switches now, even mid-swipe, and the bar always matches the screen. Fixes tabs getting stuck.
- Money: the section bar floats like the rest of the app, and pages scroll beneath it.
- Meaning search and chat model downloads work on every Wi-Fi network (some home and hotspot Wi-Fi counted as metered), with Waiting for Wi-Fi, Download failed and Retry.
- Setup: Payment alerts are part of the first-run permissions, with steps for Android’s Restricted setting. The same steps show in Money capture and Notification settings.
- Unlock & support: the note asks for your email, so your code can reach you. Codes usually unlock everything.
- Early access: everyone who starts now keeps every feature free, even if some become paid later.
- Privacy policy and Terms are linked in About and on the first screen. Feedback goes to crizo@criza.dev.
- What’s new: after each update, Crizo shows what changed, with a link to the full changelog.

## 0.9.8 (24 November 2026)

- Updates download and install from inside the app.
- Smoother tabs and sheets, Crizo colours and a simpler Unlock & support.
